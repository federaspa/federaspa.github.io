2024

Automated Document Analysis Intern at Philips

Eindhoven, Netherlands

An LLM pipeline that read 3GPP specifications, the standards behind mobile networks, and suggested improvements to them. Standards reviewers accepted 15% of its suggestions.

How it workedClose

3GPP specifications are long, dense technical documents. The pipeline took a specification and used the state-of-the-art GPT models of the time to fill in a structured improvement template for each proposed change. It was built for Philips' intellectual property and patents team, as a way to surface candidate contributions faster than reading every spec by hand.

On a dozen specifications, 15% of the suggestions were accepted by standards reviewers.

  • GPT
  • Prompt engineering
  • Structured generation
  • Python

2023 to 2024

Freelance Data Engineer for Monash University

Melbourne, Australia (remote)

An AWS pipeline that read around 100,000 company financial documents and turned them into one clean dataset, for research on whether South Africa's Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment (B-BBEE) policies worked.

How it workedClose

B-BBEE is South Africa's policy framework for encouraging Black employment. The researchers I supported wanted to measure whether it worked, and the evidence was in about 100,000 financial documents about companies. My job was to turn that pile of documents into a single, consistent dataset they could analyse.

Before settling on AWS, I also experimented with Google Cloud's Document AI.

2023

Computer Vision Intern at INRIA

Nice, France

A short internship testing whether computer vision models could follow characters through film scenes, across cuts and shot changes, for TRACTIVE, a research project on how women are represented in film.

MoreClose

To measure representation, for example how much screen time each character gets and how they're framed, you first need to know who is on screen, frame by frame. I tested YOLOv8 for detecting and tracking characters. Films are hard for trackers, which tend to assume continuous motion, because every cut breaks that assumption.

  • Object tracking
  • PyTorch
