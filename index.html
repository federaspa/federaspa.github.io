About

I studied economics, management and computer science at Bocconi before language models were everywhere. A couple of machine learning courses hooked me, so I wrote my bachelor thesis on whether social media sentiment could signal crypto price moves. (Short answer: mostly not. More on that in Research.)

I was in the middle of an MSc in Data Science and AI, learning how BERT and transformer architectures work, when ChatGPT came out. That settled it: I specialised in language models, and I've been working on them since.

Today I work on two things. The first is small language models: distilling them, post-training them and running them on-premise, so organisations get a model they can inspect and host themselves instead of sending data to a frontier API. The second is neuro-symbolic AI: pairing language models with grammars and logic solvers so their output is predictable enough for high-stakes domains like finance and regulation.
